Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Doku is developed and published by Poliglot News LLC.

Information we collect

Doku may collect information needed to operate, improve, secure, and monetize the app. This may include:

  • Gameplay and app-usage events, such as level starts, completions, restarts, and session activity
  • Device and app information, including device type, operating-system version, app version, and language
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Advertising data, including ad impressions, interactions, consent choices, and device identifiers where permitted

How we use information

We use this information to operate and improve Doku, understand gameplay, fix crashes, measure app performance, prevent fraud, and display and measure advertising.

Advertising

Doku uses Google AdMob. Depending on your location, device settings, and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Google and its advertising partners may process device, advertising, and usage information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Analytics and diagnostics

Doku may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage and diagnose technical problems.

Your choices

Where required, Doku asks for consent before using certain data for advertising or analytics. You may be able to review or change applicable privacy choices from the app's settings. You may also control tracking through your iOS privacy settings.

Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to legal, security, and operational requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Doku is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Doku changes. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are made.

Contact
