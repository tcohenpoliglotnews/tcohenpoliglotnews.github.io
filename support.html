Help with Doku

Every support email reaches a person who works on the app. Write to support@poliglotnews.com and we will reply within two business days.

Reporting a problem

If something is broken, these four details usually let us find the cause without a second exchange:

  • Your device model — for example, iPhone 14 Pro
  • Your iOS version, from Settings › General › About
  • The Doku version, shown at the bottom of the app's settings screen
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

If the problem is with a specific puzzle, tell us the level number, or the date if it was a daily board. A screenshot is worth a great deal — the board state usually tells us more than a description can.

Common questions

How do the rules work?

Every colored region on the board hides exactly one answer. The small ticks drawn inside a region say where other answers may not sit: horizontal ticks block the squares directly left and right, vertical ticks block directly above and below, and diagonal ticks block the four corners. Ticks add up, so a square showing all eight blocks every neighbor. There is a rules sheet inside the app, one tap from any board, and a fuller explanation on the Doku page.

How do I place an answer?

Double tap a square to commit it as its region's answer. A single tap only leaves a note marking a square you have ruled out — notes are free, cost you nothing, and you can press and drag to sweep them across several squares at once. If you are committing squares by accident, you are probably double-tapping when you meant to note.

Why isn't there a hint button?

Because every board is verified before it ships to be solvable by reasoning alone, a hint would only ever be doing the reasoning we designed the puzzle around. If a board feels impossible, the answer is always available somewhere on it — the rules sheet is usually the faster unstick.

I ran out of chances. Did I lose my progress?

No. Numbered levels give you three chances, and using all three only resets that attempt. Your position in the journey never moves backward, and your best result for a level you have already solved is kept. The daily puzzle has no chance limit at all — you cannot fail it, only take more moves.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Every puzzle ships inside the app, so the journey and the daily board both work fully offline. There is no account, no login, and no sync.

Will I lose my board if I close the app?

No. The board is saved on every move. Closing the app mid-puzzle, or opening settings and coming back, returns you to exactly the state you left.

How do I turn off sound, haptics, or change the theme?

Tap the cog on the home screen or on any board. Settings holds the light and dark theme, three text sizes, sound, haptics, region tags for extra color separation, and the usage-data switch.

How do I stop the app collecting usage data?

Open Settings and turn Usage data off. Collection stops immediately and any events still waiting to be sent are discarded rather than held. Advertising is handled separately by Google — you can also limit ad tracking system-wide in iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Our Privacy Policy sets out exactly what is and is not collected.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Deleting the app from your device removes everything stored locally, including your progress and the random identifier used for usage statistics. If you would also like us to delete analytics events already received, email support@poliglotnews.com and we will handle it.

Are there in-app purchases?

No. Doku has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no premium currency. Nothing in the app is behind a payment.

When is Doku coming to the App Store?

It is still in development. We have not announced a release date, and we would rather ship it right than ship it on a date. The Doku page will be updated when it is available.
