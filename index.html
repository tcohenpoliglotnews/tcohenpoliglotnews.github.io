Doku In development

A logic puzzle built on a single, quiet idea: every colored region on the board hides exactly one answer, and the marks inside each region tell you where other answers may not sit.

There are no numbers to add and no vocabulary to memorize. A board is a grid divided into colored regions, and each region is stamped with small directional ticks — horizontal, vertical, diagonal, or a combination. Those ticks are the whole rule set. Find the one square in each region that satisfies every mark on the board, and the puzzle opens up.

Doku ships with 60 hand-tuned levels across six chapters, from gentle 4×4 boards up to dense 9×9 ones, plus a new daily puzzle for every day of the season. Every single puzzle has been machine-verified before shipping to have exactly one solution reachable by pure deduction — so a solve never comes down to a guess.

How we build

Solvable by reasoning, not luck

Before a puzzle ever reaches the app, an automated solver proves its solution is unique and a deduction engine proves it can be reached by logic alone. Anything that fails either test is thrown away rather than shipped.

Works with the network off

All the puzzle content ships inside the app. There is no account to create, no login, no cloud sync, and no server call needed to play. Your progress lives on your device.

Nothing to buy

Doku has no in-app purchases, no currency, no lives to refill, and no paywall between you and the next level. The app is supported by occasional advertising, held back from new players entirely.
