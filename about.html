About

Poliglot News LLC

An independent software company in New York City, founded in 2026, building original logic and language games for iOS.

What we do

Poliglot News LLC designs and develops its own software rather than doing contract work. Everything we publish is built in-house, from the puzzle generation and the solvers that verify it, through the interface and artwork, to the App Store release itself.

Our interest is in games with small rule sets and deep consequences — the kind you can teach someone in thirty seconds and still be thinking about a week later. Our first product, Doku, is a logic puzzle built on three directional rules and a single constraint: one answer per region. It is currently in development for iPhone.

How we work

Three commitments shape most of the decisions we make, and they are worth stating plainly because they cost us things.

A puzzle should never require a guess. Every board we ship is checked by an exhaustive solver for a unique solution and replayed by a deduction engine that uses only techniques the game itself teaches. Boards that fail either check are discarded rather than patched. Generating puzzles this way is slower and throws away a great deal of work, and we think it is the whole point.

The app should work with the network off. All content ships inside the app. There is no account to create, no login, no cloud sync, and no server round-trip needed to play a level. Progress is stored on the device and belongs to the person holding it.

We do not build habits we would not want ourselves. There are no in-app purchases, no premium currency, no lives to wait out, and no paywall between a player and the next level. Advertising supports the app, and it is deliberately held back — never during a new player's first five levels, and never within a minute of real play since the last one.

Who we are

Poliglot News LLC is a limited liability company registered in the United States and operating from New York City. It was founded in 2026 by Tamir Cohen, who leads its product and engineering work.

We are small on purpose. Every email sent to our support address reaches a person who works on the app.
